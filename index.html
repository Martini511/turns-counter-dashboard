Serial log

Connect the counter over USB and the page reads its serial stream at 115200 baud. Binary packets carry the average and peak current of a 2 ms window, plain lines carry the angle. The sleep timeout is written to the device as T<ms>; the device confirms it with OK sleep. The first angle after connecting counts as the handle's rest position – connect with the handle released, or press “Zero handle” to take the reading of that moment as the new zero.

This browser does not support the Web Serial API. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera over https:// or localhost.
